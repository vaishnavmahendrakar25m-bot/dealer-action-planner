# Judge questions and honest answers

1. **Why these weights?** Demonstration assumptions from CONTEXT §8.1; no claim that they are calibrated industry benchmarks.
2. **Why not use sales alone?** Inventory, payment and service metrics can reveal concerns when sales meet target (§2).
3. **What makes an urgent case?** A severe unresolved complaint or non-disputed overdue ratio at least20%, independent of the weighted score (§8.2).
4. **How do you know a pattern is concerning?** We do not claim intent or predictive accuracy. It is a pattern worth checking, supported by records and alternative explanations (§10).
5. **What if data is missing?** Mark incomplete and request data correction; never silently reweight (§8.4).
6. **How are small peer groups handled?** Suppress peer conclusions below five dealers of the same market type (§8.3).
7. **How do you protect a dealer in a weak market?** Apply the explicit market-context condition and show raw versus adjusted scores (§9).
8. **How are disputes treated?** Exclude disputed overdue from adjusted payment risk and the urgent payment rule; show the dispute separately (§9).
9. **What about new dealers?** Use the specified ramp target and show growth only as context (§9/A5).
10. **What stops automatic punitive action?** The app only records recommendations and manager decisions. It cannot change credit or contact dealers (§4).
11. **Are transfer benefits guaranteed?** No. They are carrying-cost scenarios under stated assumptions, and can be negative (§13).
12. **Does the app use AI?** No external AI. Scores, summaries and recommendations are deterministic (§15/A11).
13. **How would production data arrive?** Weekly validated extracts from dealer, finance and service systems. Live connectors and prototype CSV loading are outside the deadline scope (§20).
14. **How do actions get followed up?** Saved baselines, owners, due dates, score-change verdicts and deferral return rules (§6.5).
15. **How would you validate this?** Compare against real historical cases, review false positives with managers, calibrate only with evidence, and validate data quality before operational use (§16/18).

Three limitations to admit: synthetic data; unvalidated demonstration thresholds and patterns; transfer feasibility and economics depend on pending real-world conditions.
